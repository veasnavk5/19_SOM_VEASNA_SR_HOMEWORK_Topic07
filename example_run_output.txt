Available tools:
- search_products: Search the product catalog by a free-text query (matches name/category).
- check_stock: Check current stock quantity for a specific product id.
- buy_product: Purchase a quantity of a product. Fails if insufficient stock.
- delete_product: Remove a product from the catalog permanently. Admin only.
